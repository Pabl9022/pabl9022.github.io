💡 What is ChatPCBridge?

ChatPCBridge is a friendly Windows application that helps you take your WeChat chat history and use it with ChatGPT. Imagine being able to ask questions about past conversations, search for key moments, or summarize long chats — all with the power of artificial intelligence.

In short: ChatPCBridge creates a simple bridge between your WeChat archives and ChatGPT, opening up new ways to interact with your chats.

🛠️ How It Works

ChatPCBridge operates in the background as a smart tool. When you have WeChat chat archives on your PC, the app organizes and prepares them for ChatGPT through a secure connection. You can then use ChatGPT to ask questions like:

  • "What did we discuss about the project last week?"
  • "Summarize this conversation from yesterday."
  • "When did we agree to meet?"

The app bridges the gap, making your old chats searchable and interactive.
